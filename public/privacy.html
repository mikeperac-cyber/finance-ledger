Equinox Ledger — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 · Microsoft Store & Desktop Compliance

1. Zero Data Collection & Local-Only Storage

Equinox Ledger is a strictly local-first personal and solo-business financial application. We do not collect, transmit, store, or process any personal information, financial records, account balances, or usage analytics on external servers.

2. Where Your Data Lives

  • Local Storage: All profiles, hourly rate settings, accounts, commitments, transactions, and cooling-off items are stored exclusively on your local device (in browser localStorage or in %LocalAppData%\EquinoxLedger for the Windows Desktop app).
  • Manual Backups: When you export a CSV, JSON tax report, or .equinox.json vault file, the file is written directly to the folder you choose on your computer.

3. No Third-Party Analytics or Advertising

Equinox Ledger contains no advertising SDKs, no telemetry trackers, no crash-reporting beacons, and no mandatory cloud accounts.

4. Data Deletion & User Control

You have complete ownership of your financial data at all times. You can permanently erase all locally stored data at any time by clicking Clear all stored data in the application sidebar or by uninstalling the desktop application.

5. Contact & Source Verification

Equinox Ledger is open-source and verifiable at github.com/mikeperac-cyber/finance-ledger .
